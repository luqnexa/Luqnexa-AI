Changes: Japanese attributed sentences were filtered, deduplicated and partitioned
by text hash; training sentences were converted into character n-gram counts,
then low-frequency features were pruned. No endorsement is implied.
context-training.json records the downloaded source hash and model hash.
The trained public model context-public.bin is distributed with this attribution.
Sentences are volunteer contributions; grammatical quality is not guaranteed.
