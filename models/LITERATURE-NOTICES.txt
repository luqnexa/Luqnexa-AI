Literary training sources for NextIME

Aozora Bunko: selected Japanese original works whose work and person copyright
flags are both "none" in the official index. Translations and other contributor
roles are excluded in this allowlist. Handling rules:
https://www.aozora.gr.jp/guide/kijyunn.html
This is not a claim that every work in Aozora Bunko is free of copyright.
Original author/title, input and proofreading credits, bibliographic colophons,
source URLs and downloaded-source hashes are preserved in literature-training.json.

Standard Ebooks: selected original English works by Jane Austen (d. 1817),
Charles Dickens (d. 1870), and Lewis Carroll (d. 1898), published before 1900.
Underlying works were reviewed for use in Japan; this is not a worldwide legal
guarantee. Standard Ebooks editorial contributions are dedicated under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Each ebook's original license statement and source URL are retained in the JSON.
The text/artwork distinction is respected; artwork is not used for training.
Author date references: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1342
https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/46 and https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/11
Project Gutenberg was researched, but no bulk download from its main site is used.

Changes: extract body text, remove ruby and notation/colophons/headings, normalize
whitespace, divide into sentences and <=160-character fragments, filter short
fragments, deduplicate, split by book, count and prune character n-gram features.
No modernization of spelling or factual correction. No endorsement is implied.
Only train-partition books contribute to context-literature.bin. Held-out books
are used for validation/testing. See literature-training.json for exact scope.
Third-party underlying text is not relicensed AGPL. Importer/model code is AGPL.
